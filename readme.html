🧱 The "Idiot-Proof" Workflow

1 Upload Your Data: Click the Browse Files button in the main window and select your store.xml file exported from BrickLink.

2 Set Your Density: Look at the sidebar. If you want Totally Empty slots, set Max Qty to 0. If you want to find drawers to top up, set it to 5 or 10.

3 Pick Your Purity: If you are holding a handful of Used parts, change the Condition Focus to "Used Only." The app will hide all your New stock so you don't make a mistake.

🎨 Reading the Visual Grid

When looking at Boxes (30 holes) or Cases (18 holes), the colors tell the story:

GREEN = PERFECT! This slot matches your filter (e.g., it's empty).
YELLOW = ALMOST EMPTY! This slot has parts, but less than your "Max Qty" limit.
FADED RED = FULL! This slot is occupied or has the wrong condition.

📁 Managing Storage Profiles

You don't need to re-type your drawer counts every day. Use the Profile Commander:

  • To Save: Type a name (like "Main Wall") in the box and click 💾 SAVE PROFILE.
  • To Switch: Use the Load Profile dropdown at the top of the sidebar.
  • To Edit: Click any folder in the Layout Editor to change prefixes or hole counts.

👻 The "Ghost" Fix (Dubious Ownership)

If the app stops updating or gives you a dubious ownership error on your network drive, copy this line, paste it into your black command prompt window, and hit Enter:

git config --global --add safe.directory "*"
